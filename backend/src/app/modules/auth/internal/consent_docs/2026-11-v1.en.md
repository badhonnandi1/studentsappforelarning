# Guardian consent (draft v1)

> DRAFT: placeholder text pending the legal review (decision D4). Do not publish as final.

You are the parent or guardian of a student under 18 who wants to use this study app.

By confirming with the code sent to your phone, you agree that:

1. The app may keep your child's name, class, institution, district and study activity to run
   practice, exams and progress reports.
2. Your child's profile is private by default. On leaderboards only the first name and the
   institution are shown, and you can turn that off at any time.
3. Your phone number is stored encrypted and used only for consent, safety and account notices.
4. You may ask for a copy of the data or its deletion through the app or support.
5. You can withdraw this consent through support; the account then needs consent again.
