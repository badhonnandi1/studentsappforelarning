# 20 · Risk register

**Date:** 2026-11-27 · **Review cadence:** at every build exit, and weekly during B5 and B6. Each risk is owned by a role (names are assigned by the engineering manager). Dates in "Trigger / decision" use weeks from the start of the build programme and the v2 decision IDs D1–D9.

**Scoring.**

- Likelihood (L) and Impact (I) each run from 1 to 5. Score = L × I.
- Score 15 or more is **red**, reviewed weekly and blocking the affected build's exit until mitigated.
- Score 8–14 is **amber**, reviewed at each build exit.
- Score 7 or less is **green**.

Every likelihood and impact value is a judgement **[Est]**. None has been measured.

**Categories:** technical · security · scalability · delivery · external decision · data/content · compliance.

## 3. Risk handling rules

- A red risk tied to a build blocks that build's exit until its mitigation is implemented and tested. Accepting the risk instead requires a supervisor-signed waiver.
- New risks found during a build are added here in the same PR as the build report.
- A risk is closed only with evidence: a test, a measurement or a recorded decision. Its row then links to that evidence.
